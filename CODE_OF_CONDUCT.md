# Community Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a technically rigorous and welcoming place for protection engineers, researchers, educators, students, and software contributors.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal attributes.
- State assumptions, units, sign conventions, and limitations clearly.
- Treat safety-critical claims with care and request validation evidence.
- Accept review professionally and give actionable feedback.
- Respect confidential, proprietary, and vendor-restricted information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, publication of private information, or pressure to present unvalidated simulation logic as field-ready protection logic is not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.
